# Single-game corroboration across time

Registered after the fixed-rival and Fisher studies, before seeing any outputs from this experiment. The full v2fin4_f model nominates the two highest candidates independently on each query. Evaluate whether a high score relying on one part of the same game signals a mistaken nomination.

## Observations and perturbations

Use candidate-only games in pub2 and priv2, 600 players with ten games each. Keep the existing 7 reference / 1 fit / 2 evaluation game roles on splits 1 and 3. Use archived, pre-pool-adaptation seed-0 members of the o12F and o33F families as two frozen probes. Verify the native full readout against the archived intact vectors for every candidate, minimum cosine 0.999 and maximum absolute discrepancy 0.012. Record hashes and ingestion counts. Keep all official question games out of scoring and fitting.

Allocate chronological two-ply units from the model's observed prefix (up to 256 plies) to four approximately equal consecutive quarters. The anonymized subject, original position numbers and board features stay fixed. Remove one quarter's keys from attention in every transformer layer. The control shuffles the quarter labels of complete two-ply units using a key-specific deterministic seed and retains the final singleton, preserving the exact count of plies of each colour under normal alternating play. Compare the intact and eight masked views. These are readout perturbations on the original board history. Subsequent boards and local windows still encode consequences of prior play.

## Specialist and controls

For each frozen probe, embed seven intact reference games per candidate and unit-normalize their mean. Standardize the 600 cosine scores per query and view, then average the two probes' scores. Let the floor be each finalist's minimum score after a chronological quarter is hidden. The primary feature is the winner-minus-runner-up floor gap minus the intact gap. Add this single correction to the accepted baseline margin, using the established nonnegative anchored one-parameter logistic fit and the established baseline scale. Fit on slot zero with the winner and runner-up both excluded from the evaluated finalists' reference-owner folds. Truth is used only on fitting rows with one finalist correct, and for reporting. Report all evaluation queries including those with neither finalist correct. Compare a size-matched shuffled floor residual, the intact probe gap alone, and the signed weakest surviving pair margin (the minimum of four margins when all favor the leader, maximum when all favor the runner-up, zero otherwise) as secondary arms. The latter tests whether broad agreement itself can rerank the contenders. Also evaluate that same margin under shuffled deletions.

Primary comparison is mean single-game competition score change over both splits combined against v2fin4_f. Bootstrap 5,000 times within pool at query-owner level, keeping all observations of one owner together. Report split-wise effects, rescued and broken first choices, and primary minus sham. The localized-dependence diagnosis compares excess winner collapse (winner loss minus runner loss under chronological deletion, less the shuffled value) in mistaken versus correct top-two decisions after matching correct cases to the error distribution in pool, seed and baseline-gap quintile. Freeze fit-slot quintile cuts. Report common support, a clustered interval, and the number of unanimous reversals in both probes together. Report labels for uncorrected secondary comparisons. Explore confirmation on splits 0 and 2 if a prespecified arm attains +0.002 with positive lower 95% bound. Full adoption also requires the existing competition-mix and robustness gates.
