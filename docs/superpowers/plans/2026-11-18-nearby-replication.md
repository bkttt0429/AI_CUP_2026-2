# Independent replay of local reply distance and exchange duration

This follow-up was registered after seeing the five-response finalist study, where nearby reply was the single promising channel. Replicate it on other games and players, and test a prespecified extension. Leave the original study and its decisions frozen.

- Use at most 600 fold-4 training-corpus players with ten games in the frozen fold-4 sequence embedding file. Exclude the player IDs and full move histories in the original choice calibration/candidate, reference-policy, and local-choice manifests. Sort eligible owners and games by fixed SHA256 order, then skip physically duplicated games before assigning slots. If fewer than 600 eligible players survive use all, requiring at least 300. Seven references and three independent single-game queries. No supervised fitting, tuning, or official question data. The fold-4 retrieval checkpoint is a single older encoder evaluated outside its training fold. The official ensemble has no equivalent vectors on this cohort.
- Nominate first and second place from the entire new roster using cosine of the single query vector and each player's mean of seven reference embeddings, normalized after averaging. The primary mechanism comparison is on queries whose owner is one of those two. In a secondary stress test use every query and the highest-scoring incorrect player of its owner's labelled rank. Labels are used only to evaluate candidate contrasts and for that diagnostic. Report roster, rank/top-two coverage and exact changes in paired evidence, in natural-log units per eligible decision.
- The single binary response is whether the subject plays within Chebyshev distance two of the last opposing stone. Keep the earlier five pre-move tactical situations, phase boundaries, simple-ko replay, first 256 plies, excluding the first twelve plies and previous passes. Fit marginal and context-conditional Bernoulli rates for each reference set using the original shrinkage 32. Priors come only from other enrolled players' reference games, excluding both candidates. Evaluate signed true-minus-rival conditional advantage over marginal, and over a matched within-game/phase shuffled reference context. Both paired 95% owner-bootstrap lower bounds must be positive to replicate the context-specific lead.
- The new temporal measurement is the number of consecutive nearby transitions among moves already played, capped at two: zero, one, or at least two. A pass resets the count. For each of the 15 context-by-streak cells, shrink the candidate rate by 32 decisions toward the population cell log odds plus the candidate's five-context log-odds offset. Compare this with the five-context model and with a control that shuffles reference streak labels within game, phase, and tactical context. Both paired lower bounds must be positive for a distinct temporal signal. Current query action cannot enter its own streak. No thresholds or weights selected after reading the new cohort.
- Fit-free standalone pair choices are descriptive. Neither a signed likelihood advantage nor success on the fold-4 retriever suffices to replace the competition ensemble. Bootstrap players together with their three queries, 5,000 draws. Save the selected IDs and role/game fingerprints locally and verify the sign and support from individual decisions. This cohort is new to these specialist experiments; existing corpus models may have been evaluated historically on its fold.
